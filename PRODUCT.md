# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A small internal team of developers and ops people who need to compose, preview, and send faxes via the YOUR_FAX_ENDPOINT_HERE API. They are technically comfortable, working at a desk, under normal office conditions. They care about getting the fax right before it goes out: correct layout, correct content, correct recipient.

## Product Purpose

A focused tool for authoring fax content (as HTML) and dispatching it through the YOUR_FAX_ENDPOINT_HERE API. The primary job: compose a fax, see exactly how it will look, attach images if needed, and send. Success means the user can go from idea to sent fax in under two minutes with full confidence in what was transmitted.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, capable. Like a professional tool that respects your time. The interface stays out of the way until you need it.

## Anti-references

- Dated enterprise software: heavy borders, gray tables, crowded toolbars
- "Hacker" terminal aesthetics: glowing green on black, CRT effects, retro chrome
- SaaS gradient overload: purple-to-blue blobs, glassy floating cards, decorative gradients

## Design Principles

1. **Compose is the center**: the fax layout preview holds visual priority; controls orbit it.
2. **See before you send**: a live preview is always present; sending without seeing is never the default flow.
3. **HTML is first-class**: the editor and preview are two views of the same document, not a textarea and a hidden popup.
4. **Images flow in**: images are content placed within the fax layout, not attachments added after.
5. **Surface depth on demand**: API parameters and advanced options appear when needed, not by default.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Best-effort. No formal WCAG compliance required, but keyboard navigation and readable contrast are standard practice.
